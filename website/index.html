How prizes work

Perfect Pick Rules

The active rule set for each prize period is shown on the Prizes tab.

Late entries and missed deadlines

Late-entry scoring applies from Week 9. Weeks 1–8 keep their original results, standings, champions and prize outcomes.

You may submit your first weekly entry while the Form remains open. The opening game's hard cutoff is one hour before its official kickoff. Each later game's official kickoff is its cutoff. A submission at the cutoff is on time.

Games whose cutoff passed before submission earn 0 points and appear crossed out with MISSED DEADLINE. Upcoming games remain eligible. Missing cutoff evidence is labelled UNVERIFIED CUTOFF.

No resubmissions or pick changes after the weekly deadline. Your latest valid on-time entry is retained; if you have none, your first valid late entry counts. Reopening the Form does not reset any deadline.

Accuracy is correct eligible picks divided by eligible submitted picks in FINAL games. Missed deadlines and unverified cutoffs are excluded. With none completed, accuracy shows an em dash. A Perfect Week requires every game to be eligible, selected, FINAL and correct.

A late entrant’s monthly tiebreaker prediction counts only from their retained first entry, submitted at or before the designated game’s official kickoff. Later submissions cannot change it. Missing kickoff evidence leaves eligibility unverified.

Picks, predictions and voting stay private until the week is independently verified CLOSED. Only eligible picks count in voting.

New Rules

3.0

Weekly entry: $5 per active player.

Perfect Pick: Pick every game correctly for the week.

If one player gets a Perfect Pick: That player wins the full weekly pot.

If multiple players get a Perfect Pick: They split the weekly pot evenly.

If nobody gets a Perfect Pick: The full weekly pot rolls into that prize period's Monthly Pot.

At the end of the prize period: 50% of the remaining Monthly Pot becomes the 1st-place monthly prize pool, and 50% moves into the End-of-Season Pot.

Monthly standings payout: The 50% monthly share goes to one 1st-place winner.

Season payout: The End-of-Season Pot keeps accumulating. The final payout structure (for example Top 3 or Top 5) will be decided later.

Monthly 1st-place tie: Tied 1st-place players answer a separate combined-points question for the final game of the prize period (WK 4, 8, 12, 16, or 18). The unique closest valid prediction wins. Missing or invalid predictions are ineligible; equally close valid predictions remain unresolved pending manual decision. No champion or payout is awarded while unresolved, and ranking points never change.

Weekly ties: Weekly Perfect Pick ties still split the weekly pot evenly. Season payout/tie rules will be decided later.

Old Rules

LEGACY

Weekly entry: $5 per active player.

Perfect Pick: A player had to correctly pick every game that week to win the weekly pot.

If one player got a Perfect Pick: That player won the full weekly pot.

If multiple players got a Perfect Pick: They split the weekly pot evenly.

If nobody got a Perfect Pick: The entire weekly pot went directly into the End-of-Season Pot.

No monthly payout: Under the Old Rules there was no monthly standings prize or 50/50 monthly split.

Player help

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for using the Perfect Pick dashboard and understanding the prize tracker.

Can I enter on Thursday after the deadline?

Yes, if this is your first weekly entry and the Form is accepting responses. If the opening game's official deadline is 4:15 PM Pacific, a 4:16 PM entry misses that game. Later games remain eligible until their own kickoff cutoffs.

What if my first entry is on Friday?

The Thursday game is MISSED DEADLINE and earns 0 points. Sunday and Monday picks remain eligible if you submit at or before each game's kickoff. In a period-ending week, your retained first entry’s monthly tiebreaker prediction can count through the designated game’s official kickoff.

What if some Sunday games have already started?

Only games whose cutoffs have not passed remain eligible. For example, an 11:00 AM Pacific entry misses games that kicked off at 10:00 AM, but can count for 1:05 PM and later games. We use each game's actual official kickoff, not these example times.

Can I change picks if the Form is reopened?

No. Reopening does not reset deadlines or allow changes after the weekly deadline. Your valid on-time entry is protected; otherwise only your first valid late entry counts.

Where can I see my picks?

Open Match Center. It defaults to the current week, and you can use the week dropdown to review earlier weeks.

How do I see how everyone picked?

Once a week is officially closed, published player picks and team voting appear in Match Center. They stay private while entries are open.

When do correct and incorrect picks update?

They update as game results in the Results sheet are marked Final. Winning/correct teams receive a subtle team-color highlight.

What is a Perfect Pick?

A Perfect Pick means a player correctly selected the winner of every game included in that week's form.

Why does the Monthly Pot change?

Under the New Rules, a finalized weekly pot rolls into the Monthly Pot when nobody gets a Perfect Pick that week.

How is the monthly standings prize paid?

At the end of a prize period, 50% of the Monthly Pot is reserved for one monthly 1st-place winner and 50% moves to the End-of-Season Pot. If 1st place is tied, the tied players use the combined-points tiebreaker for the final game of that period.

What does “Projected” prize money mean?

Projected pot amounts show how much money has accumulated under the current 50/50 rules. Season player payouts are intentionally not projected yet.

Where can I see weekly, monthly, and season standings?

Use the Weekly, Monthly, and Season tabs. Weekly and Monthly include selectors so you can review earlier weeks and prize periods.

Where can I see which rule set is being used?

Open the Prizes tab. Each prize period identifies its active rule set, pool and champion status.

What is the difference between the Old and New Rules?

Both rule sets pay the weekly pot for a Perfect Pick. Under the Old Rules, a week with no Perfect Pick sends the entire weekly pot directly to the End-of-Season Pot. Under the New Rules, that weekly pot rolls into the Monthly Pot; 50% of the monthly pot is reserved for monthly 1st place and 50% goes to the End-of-Season Pot.

What happens if players are tied?

Weekly Perfect Pick winners split that week's pot evenly. A monthly 1st-place tie is decided by the combined-points tiebreaker for the final game of the period. Season payout and season tie rules will be decided later.

Why might the weekly pot change?

The weekly pot is based on the number of active players for that week multiplied by the $5 entry amount.
