# Week 9 deadline disclosure release

Owner authorization changes disclosure, not scoring. Week 9's verified opening
kickoff is 2026-11-06T01:15:00.000Z; its configured cutoff is
2026-11-06T00:15:00.000Z (November 5, 4:15 PM Pacific). Disclosure is inclusive at
that cutoff. It does not depend on every player submitting or on Form acceptance.
Weeks 1–8 retain their existing historical authorization. No later week is
implicitly authorized: adding a future week requires a separate reviewed grant and
verification of its configured cutoff and schedule.

## Public contract and strategic effect

PP4_STATIC_V2 remains backward compatible. An optional strictly validated
`deadlineDisclosures` record authorizes only Week 9. The record pins the season,
week, cutoff, opening kickoff, owner policy and fresh source observation. Existing
public field allowlists and random IDs remain mandatory. Form status is retained
truthfully, including OPEN; it is not rewritten to CLOSED. Expired snapshots are
rejected and their protected UI is cleared by the existing expiration mechanism.

Submitted picks, eligible voting and tiebreaker predictions may be retained for
this explicitly approved week after cutoff. Missing accepted entries display
Not Submitted, with no invented selections or rank. Raw submission timestamps,
contacts, source keys and identity mappings remain private. Game eligibility,
MISSED DEADLINE, accuracy, resubmission selection and all prize formulas are
unchanged.

A late first entrant can inspect others' future-game selections and tiebreaker
predictions before making their own first entry. This creates an informational
advantage and may influence strategy. The owner-approved disclosure policy permits
that effect; this release does not compensate by changing scoring or deadlines.
Reopening the Form neither resets cutoffs nor authorizes a future week.
